The applicable Data use rights shall apply with respect to the particular use case as set forth below.  

  1. Data Use Rights.
    1. Data used to train Gdocz models (“Training Data”).  Training Data includes, but is not limited to output resulting from Customer’s use of Gdocz products and services; inputs and requests submitted to Gdocz products and services; and information and Data regarding files uploaded into Gdocz products and services.  Gdocz may freely use Training Data for any purpose including but not limited to training and improving Gdocz models and Gdocz products and services.
    2. Data used for internal purposes, such as but not limited to testing and quality assurance (“Internal Use Data”).  Internal Use Data includes, but is not limited to Data regarding the performance and operation of Gdocz products and services.  Gdocz may freely use Internal Use Data to improve the performance and operation of Gdocz products and services.
    3. Data used to make improvements to Gdocz products and services, such as but not limited to new features and functions (“Improvement Data”).  Improvement Data includes, but is not limited to Data related to Customer’s use of Gdocz products and services.  Gdocz may freely use Improvement Data to make improvements to and add new features and functions to Gdocz products and services.
  2. Customer may opt out of Gdocz’s use of Training Data and Internal Use Data by indicating the same in the Gdocz dashboard located at the following URL: https://www.gramosoft.in/app/dashboard after which Gdocz will cease use of the same.

Gdocz may update this Policy from time-to-time by publishing such update and providing notice to Customer pursuant to the ToU or CA, as applicable.  The updated Policy shall apply following such notice.
